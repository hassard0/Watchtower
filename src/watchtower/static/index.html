Local identity
Friendly names & authorized decryption
Resolve names from Bluetooth Classic, paired BlueZ aliases, mDNS/DNS-SD, UPnP and DHCP. For owned devices, imported EAD, IRK, or Fast Pair account keys unlock authenticated protocol-defined names. Keys never leave this Pi and are encrypted at rest.
AirTag and Tile owner labels are not transmitted in their radio advertisements and cannot be recovered from them. Watchtower reports those devices from protocol fingerprints and accepts your own catalog labels; decryption here is limited to keys you own or obtain through authorized pairing.
Identify AirPods or another Apple audio accessory
  1. Place the accessory next to Watchtower and put it in Bluetooth pairing mode until its status light flashes white.
  2. Enter the setup token, then select scan / identify AirPods.
  3. When its disclosed name appears below, select pair to retain its trusted BlueZ alias.
